Our Story

Aroma Coffee & Tea House was established in 2015 out of a passion for good coffee and tea. Our founders wanted to create a welcoming space where people could enjoy high-quality drinks made from carefully sourced local and regional ingredients.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve exceptional coffee and tea, prepared with care, while supporting local farmers and offering every customer a warm and memorable experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the most loved coffee and tea house in the region, known for quality, sustainability and a strong sense of community.
